# Archived section background assets

These earlier photographic experiments are preserved as source assets and are no longer displayed. The four sections now use only abstract SVG/CSS backgrounds. Images below were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool and converted to WebP.

## aim/rooftop

Asset: `aim/rooftop.webp`

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-width PUBG website section background. Landscape 16:9 premium photorealistic AAA game cinematic key art, detailed realistic textures, atmospheric depth, no text, UI, logos or watermarks. Left 45% is dark uncluttered negative space for readable website copy. Main subject and interesting composition in right half, subject entirely visible. Iconic PUBG helmet is level-three black combat helmet with face visor; survivor wears white rolled sleeve shirt, loose black tie, tactical belt and jeans. No blood or gore. Iconic PUBG survivor on an Erangel rooftop on RIGHT, kneeling and aiming a scoped bolt-action rifle toward distant rooftops, profile perspective with rifle pointing toward left, orange sunset backlight, subtle floating embers and dusty air. Focus on precision and rifle silhouette.
